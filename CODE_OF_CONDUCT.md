# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and kind.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other contributors.
- Respecting different opinions.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, offensive language, and personal attacks.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the repository owner through GitHub:

GitHub: https://github.com/anubose1002-ai

## Enforcement

The repository owner is responsible for reviewing and addressing reported issues.

All contributors are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.
